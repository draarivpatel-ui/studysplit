# Design

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Mode

Operate. The visitor came to split notes and inspect a routing decision.

## Visual world

A precision study instrument. The world contributes a dark instrument palette, measured spacing, Plex type, and one signature move: two visible routing lanes whose active line lights green for on-device or blue for cloud.

## Type

IBM Plex Sans for interface copy. IBM Plex Mono only for measurements, probe state, latency, source lines, and other data.

## Palette

Near-black blue green ground, raised blue green panels, mint for local execution, blue for cloud execution, amber for warnings and focus, red only for hard difficulty or errors.

## Layout

Three working regions at desktop width: notes input, route monitor, and flashcard deck. The route monitor is not a decoration or a hidden log. It is a peer of the editing surface. Narrow screens stack the same regions in reading order.

## State rules

Every control has hover, focus, disabled, loading, and empty states. The privacy badge only appears after at least one completed call and only when every completed call used the on-device leg. Demo transport is labeled as demo wherever it appears.

## Motion

One restrained pulse on the active routing node. No scroll-linked motion, no animated entrances, and no smooth scrolling that would fight the recording driver.
